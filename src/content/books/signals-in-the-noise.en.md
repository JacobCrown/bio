A well-organised tour of ideas I mostly knew, explained better than I have seen
them explained elsewhere. The chapter on overfitting through the lens of weather
forecasting is excellent.

It loses momentum in the second half, where the examples become repetitive and the
author seems reluctant to commit to a conclusion. Good, not great — worth it for the
first four chapters alone.
