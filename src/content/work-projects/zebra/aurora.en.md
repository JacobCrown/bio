The goal of the project is to enable the end customer to train vision models on their own data and deploy them without writing code. The project consists of two separate applications: a GUI and a backend. The GUI controls the backend, which executes instructions according to a given configuration. This makes it possible to control the training process in a strictly graphical and intuitive way.
